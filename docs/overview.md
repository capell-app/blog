# Blog

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Blog lets you write and publish articles on your site, with their own blog page, monthly archives, and tag pages. You can keep an article private as a draft while you work on it, schedule it to go live later, and feature related articles in widgets on other pages.

## Your screens

- **Articles**: the list of every article, showing what is published, draft, or scheduled.
- **The article editor**: where you write an article, add a featured image, set tags, and choose when it goes live.
- **Your blog page, archive pages, and tag pages**: the public pages visitors browse.
- **Article health**: an admin panel that flags articles missing key details or translations.

## What you can do

- Write a new article and publish it.
- Save an article as a draft to keep working on it privately.
- Schedule an article to go live on a future date.
- Set a featured image and tags.
- Reuse articles elsewhere with the article and related-article widgets.

## Where to find it

Go to **Content > Articles** in the admin. Tags are set on each article as you write it.

## Good to know

- A **draft** stays private. Visitors never see it until you publish.
- A **scheduled** article appears on its own, at the date and time you set.
- Articles share your site's RSS/Atom feeds automatically, so readers can subscribe.
